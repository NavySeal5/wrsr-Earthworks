[h1]EARTHWORKS PLANNING[/h1]

Plan and schedule terrain work as editable Sites inside named Projects. Preview terrain changes, set target heights at individual points, and assign Earthworks facilities to provide machine capacity.

[h2]FEATURES[/h2]
[list]
[*]Small, Medium, and Large Earthworks facilities with 4, 8, and 20 vehicle spaces.
[*]Create multiple named Projects and manage several Sites in each Project.
[*]Draw polygon, square, and rectangular Sites. Set or sample the target height at each point, then review the terrain preview and cut/fill estimates.
[*]Start, pause, resume, and review terrain work from the Earthworks panel.
[*]Save and reload Projects, Sites, facility assignments, and work progress.
[/list]

[h2]USING PROJECTS, FACILITIES, AND SITES[/h2]
Open the Earthworks panel from the button near the lower-left corner of the screen. Create or select a Project, then create Sites in that Project.

[b]In the building menu, Earthworks facilities are listed with Depots.[/b] Only Earthworks facilities can be assigned to a Project and provide capacity for its Sites. Park bulldozers or excavators at these facilities. Only bulldozers and excavators count toward Earthworks capacity; other vehicles do not.

In the Facilities section, assign one or more Earthworks facilities to the selected Project.

A facility can belong to only one Project. A Project can have several facilities and Sites. Workers are not required. Start a Site to begin work. Within a Project, the first eligible Site in queue order uses the pooled machine capacity of its assigned facilities. Other started Sites wait until it completes. Paused Sites and Sites that need review are skipped.

[h2]SITE CREATION CONTROLS[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Select a Project and click [b]New Site[/b].
[*]Choose [b]Free draw[/b], [b]Square[/b], or [b]Rectangle[/b] before placing the first point.
[*]Optional: enable [b]Lock to Grid[/b] before placing the first point. New points then snap to the nearest terrain grid vertex.
[*]For Free draw, click boundary points in sequence. Click within 5 m of the first point or choose [b]Finish shape[/b] to close the Site. For Square or Rectangle, click one corner, move the cursor to preview the shape, then click the other corner.
[*]Name the Site. Select points to enter a target height, or click [b]Sample selected[/b] and click the map to sample a height. Use the minus or plus button to adjust selected points by 1 m. Hold Ctrl for 10 m steps. Shift-click points to add them to or remove them from the selection. Hold Shift while clicking minus or plus to adjust all points in the shape.
[*]Click [b]Save Site[/b] to add it to the Project queue. Drawing and saving only create a plan. Terrain changes begin after you start the Site.
[/olist]

Sites support up to 32 points, with at least 5 m between points. You can add a point on an edge, remove selected points while keeping at least three, or Shift-drag inside a Site to move its full footprint. Editing active work pauses the Site. Review the terrain before resuming it.

[h2]CAPACITY AND SCOPE[/h2]
Each eligible parked vehicle contributes 250 m3 per in-game day for each displayed Speed/Level point at 1x speed. The global Site speed setting scales the work rate and estimates. Optional distance contribution is off by default. It reduces facility contribution by distance, but does not set a maximum operating radius.

Terrain work does not consume fuel, money, or quarried stone. Facility fuel storage and refuelling are not included. The visible facility tank is decorative.
